Prayer companion

Original prayers inspired by the path of finding the psychic being

This small app gathers short original prayers, quiet practices, and reflective prompts. The themes come from teachings that describe the psychic being as the soul-centre deep behind the heart and speak of aspiration, sincerity, perseverance, and self-giving.

How this app is arranged

  • Prayers are grouped by inner need: aspiration, quietude, surrender, guidance, gratitude, and perseverance.
  • Each prayer is brief and original, so it can be used in personal devotion, meditation, or silent reading.
  • The practice cards suggest simple inward movements: go behind the heart, become quiet, offer the day, and listen before acting.

Simple practice

Use one of these before reading a prayer.

Go inward

Close the eyes for one minute, place attention in the middle of the chest, and imagine moving gently behind the surface emotions.

Offer the day

Name one worry, one desire, and one action. Offer all three inwardly before beginning work or rest.

Listen quietly

After reading a prayer, keep silence for a few breaths and notice the smallest truthful indication rather than the loudest impulse.
